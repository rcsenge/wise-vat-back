The problem

  • Your team travels for business.
  • A hotel in Berlin, Vienna, or Warsaw.
  • They pay the bill. On top: VAT, the sales tax.
  • That tax is not the hotel's. It is the government's.
  • But the company paid for a business trip.
  • The company can claim it back. European law. An EU VAT refund.

The catch: almost nobody claims it. The paperwork. And the rules change per country.

03:15

Why it is hard

  • Rules differ per country.
    • Austria: no VAT back on fuel.
    • France: none on a hotel room.
    • Poland: half on fuel.
  • Ten categories. Nine countries. Different answers.
  • Match each receipt to a card payment. One invoice, one transaction, right country, right date, right amount.
  • Deadline. 30 September of the next year. Miss it. Gone.
  • Result: a finance person, a shoebox of paper, working it out by hand.

Safe point: “Look at the screen. That passes during this talk.”

€20,000,000,000 a year left on the table.

02:30

The solution

SIGINT makes it automatic. You do the trip. It does the rest.

  1. Employee uploads a receipt. A PDF.
  2. The app reads it. The supplier, the VAT number, the amounts, the date.
  3. The app checks it. Real invoice? Legit VAT number?
  4. The app matches it. To the right card payment, automatically.
  5. The app applies the country rules. Line by line. Tells you what you get back.

Every decision comes with a plain-English reason. It never says “no” silently. It tells you why.

No spreadsheets. No shoebox. No guessing.
