## 0:45 — The problem

- Team travels for business.
- Hotel in Berlin. Vienna. Warsaw.
- They pay the bill. On top: **VAT** — the sales tax.
- That tax is not the hotel's. It is the government's.
- But the company pays for a business trip.
- **The company can claim it back.** European law. EU VAT refund.

- The catch: **nobody claims it.**
- Why? Paperwork. And the rules change per country.

---

## 1:45 — Why it is hard

- **Rules differ per country.**
  - Austria → no VAT back on fuel.
  - France → none on a hotel room.
  - Poland → half on fuel.
  - Ten categories. Nine countries. Different answers.
- **Match each receipt to a card payment.** One invoice, one transaction, right country, right date, right amount.
- **Deadline.** 30 September of the next year. Miss it. Gone.
- **Result:** finance person, shoebox of paper, works it out by hand.
- Slow. Boring. Most companies **give up.**
- **€20,000,000,000 a year** left on the table.

## 2:30 — The solution

- SIGINT makes it automatic.
- You do the trip. It does the rest.

1. **Employee uploads a receipt.** Photo or PDF.
2. **The app reads it.** Pulls the supplier, the VAT number, the amounts, the date.
3. **The app checks it.** Real invoice? Legit VAT number?
4. **The app matches it.** To the right card payment. Automatically.
5. **The app applies the country rules.** Line by line. Tells you what you get back.

- Every decision comes with a plain-English reason.
- Never says "no" silently. Tells you **why.**

- No spreadsheets. No shoebox. No guessing.
